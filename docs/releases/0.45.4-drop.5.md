# Crew for Drop 0.45.4-drop.5

The guest manifest declares S3 optional. During a Drop trial, Crew runs on the app volume, keeps attachment originals there, and shows the owner a persistent storage warning. Suspend and terminate flush that volume and report replication as disabled.

On the first paid deployment, Crew reopens the existing database and reserves its replica namespace. It uploads the attachment originals created during the trial before starting Litestream. Interrupted uploads resume on the next startup. Cold recovery continues to require the original app binding and a complete replica.
